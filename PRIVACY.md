# Shadow Hunter — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Shadow Hunter is a single-player game made by Autobuild Studios. This policy explains what data the game and its partners handle.

## What the game itself collects
Nothing. The game has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of its own. Your progress and settings are saved only on your device (or, on web portals, in the portal's own save system), and they're never sent to us.

## Advertising (Android app)
The Android app shows ads through **Google AdMob**. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect:
- your device's advertising ID,
- your approximate location (from your IP address),
- device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from **Settings → Privacy options** in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google's use of this data is described at <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>, and Google's privacy policy is at <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

## Web portals (CrazyGames, Playgama)
When you play on a web portal, that portal's own SDK handles ads, saves and any account features under the portal's privacy policy. The game itself doesn't collect anything there either.

## Children
The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.
